# Content
Topic: **Neuroscience**

## Overview/Introduction/Context
* Simply put, neuroscience is the study of the nervous system, ranging from how the nervous system develops to its structure and what it does. It covers the whole nervous system, with a primary focus on the brain. Neuroscientists focus on the brain and its impact on behavior and cognitive functions. Technology is essential to neuroscience because it allows scientists to study how the brain and nervous system function. Technologies such as MRI scans, EEGs, artificial intelligence, and brain-computer interfaces help researchers observe brain activity, understand neurological disorders, study memory and behavior, and develop treatments.

## Part A: Existing technology

##### Software
* Google Search: "Neuroscience apps"
* 1st app: **Neuronify**
  * Neuronify allows you to investigate how neurons and neural networks behave. Use it to explore connections between neurons and how changes to a single connection can affect entire networks and lead to behavioural changes.
  * Available on Google Play and the Apple App Store
  * It is free
  * Users can learn about neurons and neural networks in the brain.
  * Allows the user to easily create and explore neural networks in a plug-and-play simulation environment.
  * Designed for iPad. Not verified for macOS.
  * Available on Windows and iOS.
  * https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&url=https%3A%2F%2Fovilab.net%2Fneuronify%2F2017%2F06%2F15%2Fneuronify-1-2-released%2F&ved=0CBcQjRxqFwoTCPDE673N2JcDFQAAAAAdAAAAABA7&opi=89978449
  * It is good for beginners who want to learn, but not for actual research data.

* Google search: "Neuroscience software"
* 2nd software: Allen Institute for Brain Science
  * https://brain-map.org
  * Explore real brain structures and research data.
  * Users can investigate:
      * Where different brain regions are located.
      * Which types of cells exist in different regions.
      * Which genes are expressed in different brain areas.
      * How different brain structures are organized.
      * What researchers have discovered through experiments.
  * It is free
  * Runs on all hardwares
  * Logo: (https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&url=https%3A%2F%2Fbrain-map.org%2F&ved=0CBcQjRxqFwoTCOD-w77S55cDFQAAAAAdAAAAABA-&opi=89978449)
    
* 3rd software: 3D Slicer
  * https://download.slicer.org
  * It is free
  * Open-source medical imaging program used to visualize, process, and analyze medical images, including MRI and CT scans of the brain.
  * Neuroscientists use brain imaging to investigate brain structures, development, and changes associated with neurological conditions.
  * The software allows users to examine real brain scans and better understand the physical organization of the brain.
  * 3D Slicer runs on modern Windows, macOS, and a variety of Linux distributions.
  * logo: https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdownload.slicer.org%2F&ved=0CBcQjRxqFwoTCJCcjKLS55cDFQAAAAAdAAAAABA9&opi=89978449
