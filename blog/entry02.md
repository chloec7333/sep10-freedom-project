## Research Software and Hardware & Synthesis Material
For this entry, we had to research the problem, which for me is the field of neuroscience. We first started with 2 different categories, software and hardware. 

#### Software
For software, I first searched up ¨Neuroscience apps¨. I looked through some websites and I found 2 that stood out to me.  The first app I found was **Neuronify**, which allows users to learn about neurons and neural networks in the brain and the user is easily create and explore neural networks in a plug-and-play simulation environment. I found this app while looking through [Best mobile apps for neuroscientists](https://www.scientifica.uk.com/neurowire/best-mobile-apps-for-neuroscientists) by Scientifica, which is a webpage with a list of apps that are available for neuroscientists. The second app I found is called 3D Brain. This is a open-source medical imaging program used to visualize, process, and analyze medical images, including MRI and CT scans of the brain. I found this on a website, [Top 10 Neurology Apps](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/top-10-neurology-apps?slide=10) by Neurology Live. I continued to search for other apps and softwares but was not finding what I really wanted, so I refined my search to be "Neuroscience software". I looked through a couple websites, and found one that I thought was useful and informative about the Neuroscience field, which is the Allen Institute for Brain Science. The software component of this website is [the brain map](https://brain-map.org), where users can explore real brain structures and research data.
