## Choosing Topic (Neuroscience)
The topic I have chosen year long project is neuroscience. The reason I have chosen neuroscience is because personally, I think the study of the brain and nervous system is very interesting and it's the reason how everyone is functioning right now. Before I started researching for topics, I wasn't really interested into, especially for my career. I decided to randomly look into topics I thought sounded interesting and started researching about them. I narrowed the topics into my top 3 which were health care, neurology, and astronomy. Though I didn't pick health care and astronomy, I still think they are interesting topics and I found some interesting articles along the way while I was researching. Some articles I found on these topic are [8 Example of Health Information Technology and their Uses](https://c8health.com/blog/exploring-the-types-of-health-information-technology-and-their-uses) by C8 Health, [Astronomy](https://www.britannica.com/science/astronomy/The-techniques-of-astronomy) by Britannica, [Emerging Healthcare Technology: What’s Next in Healthcare?](https://nevadastate.edu/son/rn-bsn/emerging-healthcare-technology-whats-next-in-healthcare/) by Nevada State University, and [First AI-driven telescope goes stargazing](https://phys.org/news/2026-07-ai-driven-telescope-stargazing.html#google_vignette) by Amanda Morris, Northwestern University. It was interesting to research and see how technology is applied to these fields. 

For neurology, it's a little complicated since I sort of changed to neuroscience toawrds the end after my class decided their topics because I discovered something while I was revisiting neurology. During the first time I was researching neurology, I had assumed that nueroscience and nuerology was the same thing and didn't research them seperately. Both study pretty much the same area of subject, the central nervous system and human brain function, but there are a few distinct differences, because neurology is more concentrated on the medical side. How I decided between nuerology and neuroscience was by thinking what I would be more interested researching in for the whole school year and possibly have as a career, and I ultimately decided on neuroscience because it goes beyond the medical field and I'm not sure if I want to have a career in the medical field yet. The article [Neurology vs Neuroscience: What's the Difference?](https://www.northcentralcollege.edu/news/2022/09/16/neurology-vs-neuroscience-whats-difference) by Noth Central College also helped me understand the differences and come up with a decision. An article I found interesting for neuroscience is [Neurotechnologies: The Next Technology Frontier](https://brain.ieee.org/topics/neurotechnologies-the-next-technology-frontier/) by IEEE Brain. It's amazing to see how technology has advanced so much in this field that now we can answer questions about how our brain functions even if we can't physically see it.
